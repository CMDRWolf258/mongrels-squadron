Your First Steps

Start Here

Elite Dangerous is enormous. You do not need to learn all of it at once. Pick the description that sounds most like you, take a few useful steps, and discover what you actually enjoy.

One Rule for This Page

Do the next useful thing — not everything.

These are suggested routes, not gates. If you already know something, skip it. If an activity looks interesting, jump straight into it. The goal is to give you a clear next step without hiding the depth of the rest of the site.

Choose Your Starting Point

Where are you right now?

You can switch tracks whenever you want. These simply change how much context we put in front of you at once.

01

New to Elite

You are still getting comfortable with your ship, credits, modules, travel, or simply figuring out what the game expects from you.

  • Get comfortable launching, docking, navigation and your rebuy cost.
  • Complete a few missions and build a small credit cushion.
  • Upgrade one ship instead of chasing a perfect end-game build.
  • Try one activity that sounds interesting.
  • Fly with another Mongrel when you are ready.
Give Me Something to Do
02

I Know the Basics

You can get around the galaxy and make credits. Now you want to understand more systems, improve a ship, or find an activity worth getting good at.

  • Choose one activity to explore more deeply.
  • Start learning Engineering and what your build actually needs.
  • Join a squad activity instead of learning everything solo.
  • Learn how the Mongrels use Open Play and squad tasking.
  • Build toward a goal rather than buying modules at random.
Browse Activities
03

Experienced Commander

You know Elite already. You mainly need to understand how the Mongrels operate, where current tasking lives, and where to find the specialist material you care about.

  • Review squad rules and the Open Play standard.
  • Check Mission Control and current Daily Orders.
  • Create or update your member profile and specialties.
  • Browse the Field Manual only where you need a reference.
  • Use the Member Portal as your private launch point.
