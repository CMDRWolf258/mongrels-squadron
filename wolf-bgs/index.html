Reward AdministrationPer-order member payout defaults · manual adjustments will post to the audited reward ledger+

Reward caps are personal and per individual Daily Order. A squad order reaching its operational goal does not close reward eligibility for other members or for additional qualifying work up to that member's cap.

Trade Profit Every complete profit block earns the configured matching reward. Default: each 10M Cr of verified profit earns 10M Cr, capped at 30M Cr per member for that order.
Mission INF Each verified INF earns the full rate until that order's squad goal is reached. Additional personal INF remains reward-eligible at the reduced rate until the member reaches the larger of the order goal or the minimum cap.
Bounty Vouchers Verified redeemed vouchers earn the configured matching rate. The operational goal can be met while members continue earning from the same order until their own cap is reached.
Order-scoped caps Each published order has its own personal cap. Two separate 20M bounty orders therefore provide up to 30M + 30M = 60M Cr of reward opportunity per member under the defaults. Progress on one order never consumes the other order's cap.
Manual adjustments The payout ledger will provide one central adjustment form: select the authenticated member, enter a positive or negative M Cr amount, require a reason, and optionally attach it to an order. Adjustments will create ledger entries; balances will never be silently overwritten.
