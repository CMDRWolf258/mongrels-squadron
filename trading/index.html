Priority & Freshness Profiles

Each watch interprets the same observation timestamp according to its assigned profile. Enter hours and minutes directly; minute overflow is normalized automatically. Refresh intervals cannot be set below 5 minutes.

CriticalFast-moving CGs / rapidly depleting markets
HighImportant short-lived opportunities
StandardNormal trade routes and routine market leads
LowLong-term or low-volatility watches

Example: entering 90 in a minutes box becomes 1 hr 30 min automatically. Critical defaults to Fresh ≤30 min, Aging ≤1 hr 30 min. Standard defaults to Fresh ≤24 hr, Aging ≤48 hr. Anything older than the Aging cutoff is Stale.
