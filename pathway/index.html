Tune Your Pathway

What matters to you?

Select only the areas you care about. “Interested” means you enjoy or want to see the activity. “Want to improve” gives it extra priority. Your experience band changes the kind of work you receive: beginners acquire capability, developing pilots practice it, experienced pilots demonstrate it, and veterans are pushed toward harder challenges, teamwork, and teaching.

Private to You
Your selections are private and only used to personalize your Mongrel experience.

Your Pathway

What should I work on next?

Full pathways use different assignment types — Learn, Build, Demonstrate, Challenge, Wing / Team, and Teach / Mentor. The site picks an experience-appropriate route, but it will not explain every hidden prerequisite. Researching the details is part of the development; Ask the Mongrels is there when you get stuck.
